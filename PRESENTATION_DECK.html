The Challenge

The Digital Payment Fraud Crisis

High Velocity

Sub-Second Settlements

Instant payment rails like UPI and IMPS transfer funds within 200 milliseconds. Once stolen, funds leave the banking perimeter before audits can even trigger.

Organized Crime

Multi-Hop Mule Rings

Scammers don't keep stolen funds in one place. They rapidly funnel amounts through 4 to 6 "money mule" accounts in circles to evade anti-money laundering (AML) monitors.

Operational Deficit

Alert Fatigue & False Alarms

Legacy rule thresholds trigger thousands of false alarms, blocking legitimate users while black-box AI tools fail to explain why a transaction was stopped.

The Innovation

SmartSpend: Dual-Stage Pre-Settlement Interception

What SmartSpend Does Differently

SmartSpend acts as an automated AI Security Guard positioned directly between payment gateways and bank core ledgers:

  • Instant Scoring: Computes calibrated 0–100 risk score in <50ms.
  • Pre-Settlement Hold: Pauses high-risk transfers before money leaves the bank.
  • Explainable XAI: Gives plain-language reasons instead of raw math.
  • Indian Native: Formatted for ₹ INR, UPI VPAs, and RBI compliance.

Dynamic Decision Policies

Score 0 – 30 (Low Risk): Automated Instant Settlement
Score 31 – 70 (Medium Risk): Enhanced Step-up OTP / MFA
Score 71 – 100 (High Risk): Pre-Settlement Hold & Analyst Triage

The Money Mule Problem

Individual transactions look harmless in isolation (e.g. ₹85,000 transfer). But when mapped globally, money is circulating through multiple accounts to obscure origin:

Acc 102938 ➔ Acc 839201 (₹85.4K)
Acc 839201 ➔ Acc 492019 (₹84.0K)
Acc 492019 ➔ Acc 993847 (₹82.5K)
Acc 993847 ➔ Acc 102938 (₹81.0K)
