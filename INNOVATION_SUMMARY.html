SmartSpend

AI-Powered Financial Fraud Detection & Risk Analytics Platform

TSM-TECHNOVA 2026
Track: Fintech & Cybersecurity

1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

The rapid proliferation of instant digital payment networks (UPI, IMPS, and real-time banking) has revolutionized commerce while simultaneously empowering organized cyber-fraud syndicates. Traditional fraud verification relies on slow post-settlement audits or brittle rule engines that produce unacceptable false positive rates and zero explainability. When accounts are compromised, stolen capital is rapidly layered through multi-hop money mule accounts and cashed out within minutes. SmartSpend solves this crisis by introducing a dual-stage, pre-settlement AI intelligence platform that calculates calibrated 0–100 risk scores in under 50ms while tracking complex multi-party laundering loops across streaming ledgers.

2. Proposed Solution & Architecture

SmartSpend is an end-to-end, full-stack risk management platform providing real-time interception, explainable forensics, and decisive role-based governance:

⚡ Stage 1: Machine Learning Classifier

A high-throughput Random Forest ensemble evaluates 17 behavioral signals (ledger balance deltas, account clearing ratios, transaction-to-wealth velocity, and time-of-day dynamics) to assess individual transaction risk.

🕸️ Stage 2: Fraud Ring Network Tracker

An in-memory directed temporal graph traversal engine detects circular money laundering loops ($A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C \rightarrow A$) and smurfing funnels in under 15ms, catching syndicates invisible to isolated point classifiers.

4. Key Differentiators & Innovation Highlights

  • Zero-Black-Box Explainability: Translates complex model predictions into plain-language forensic attribution (e.g., "Account balance wiped from ₹85,400 to ₹0 in one transfer"), reducing investigation time from 20 minutes to under 5 seconds.
  • Dual-Stage Risk Synergy: Fuses isolated ledger behavioral flags with graph topological centrality for holistic syndicate interdiction.
  • Role-Based Operational Workflow: Tailored governance separating Fraud Analysts (review, triage, and OTP verification) from Fraud Managers (executive authority to freeze compromised accounts and entire criminal rings).
  • Indian Rupee (₹) & UPI Native: Fully formatted with Indian Lakh/Crore notation and calibrated for UPI, IMPS, and NEFT payment rails.
